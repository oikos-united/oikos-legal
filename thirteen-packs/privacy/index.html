Thirteen Packs Solitaire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Oikos United LLC

What This App Does

Thirteen Packs Solitaire is an offline card game. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account registration, no sign-in, and no server-side data collection by Oikos United. However, this app does display advertisements served by Google AdMob, and Google's advertising SDK collects certain device and usage data as described below.

Data We (Oikos United) Collect

Oikos United does not collect any personal information from players.

We have no backend server that receives your data. Your game progress, preferences, and any other app state exist only on your device.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores game state locally on your device using standard mobile storage (AsyncStorage). This includes:

  • Game progress (current game state, deal history)
  • App preferences (sound settings, remove-ads purchase status)

This data exists only on your device. It is never transmitted to Oikos United or any third party by us. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Advertising — Google AdMob

This app displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC.

When you use this app, Google's AdMob SDK may automatically collect the following data to serve relevant advertisements:

  • Advertising identifier — the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, or the Google Advertising ID on Android
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, screen size
  • IP address — used to determine approximate geographic location for ad targeting
  • App usage data — how you interact with the app and the ads displayed
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address (not GPS-precise location)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by Oikos United. Oikos United does not receive or store this data.

Google's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy, available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

iOS — App Tracking Transparency: On first launch (before ads load), the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. AdMob accesses your IDFA and serves personalized ads only if you allow tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but non-personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android: you can manage or reset your Advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID (Android 12+) or Opt out of Ads Personalization (earlier versions).

Remove Ads Purchase

This app offers a one-time in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") for $2.99 USD that permanently disables all advertising.

  • Purchases are processed by the platform app store — Apple (App Store / StoreKit) on iOS, or Google Play Billing on Android. Oikos United never handles or stores payment card information
  • Purchase status (ads removed: yes/no) is stored locally on your device
  • The platform's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's or Google's respective Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

If you purchase Remove Ads and later uninstall and reinstall the app, you can restore your purchase through your app-store account without paying again.

Analytics and Tracking

Oikos United does not use any analytics tools. We do not directly track your usage, session length, location, or behavioral data. The only third-party data collection in this app is Google AdMob, described above.

Children's Privacy

Oikos United does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Google AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's own policies; players and parents should review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how Google handles data from minors.

If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about data collection by the advertising SDK, you can contact Google directly or use the Remove Ads purchase to disable all advertising entirely.

Data Sharing

Oikos United shares no data with anyone because Oikos United collects no data. Google AdMob may share data it collects in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

Because Oikos United collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete from Oikos United's systems. For data collected by Google AdMob, please contact Google or refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on exercising your rights.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app introduces additional data collection or changes to the advertising arrangement, this policy will be updated before that version is released. The Play Store listing will always link to the current version of this policy.

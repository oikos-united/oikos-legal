Thirteen Packs Solitaire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Oikos United LLC

What This App Does

Thirteen Packs Solitaire is an offline card game. It runs entirely on your device. There is no account registration, no sign-in, and no internet connection required or used.

Data We Collect

We collect nothing.

Thirteen Packs Solitaire does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. There is no backend server. No data leaves your device.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores game state locally on your device using standard mobile storage (AsyncStorage on iOS and Android). This includes:

  • Game progress (current game state, deal history)
  • App preferences (any settings you configure)

This data exists only on your device. It is never transmitted to Oikos United or any third party. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any analytics tools. We do not track your usage, your session length, your location, or any other behavioral data. There are no third-party SDKs in this app that collect data.

Third-Party Services

This app uses no third-party services that collect data. There are no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash reporting services, and no social integrations.

Children's Privacy

Thirteen Packs Solitaire does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, no special COPPA provisions apply beyond this statement.

Data Sharing

We share no data with anyone because we collect no data.

Your Rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete beyond the local data on your own device, which you control and can delete by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app introduces data collection (such as cloud backup or an optional account), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and we will note the change clearly. The App Store listing will always link to the current policy.
